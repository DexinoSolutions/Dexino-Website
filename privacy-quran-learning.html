Quran Learning for Beginners

Privacy Policy

Dexino Cloud & AI Solutions · Mobile Apps · Privacy

This Privacy Policy explains how Quran Learning for Beginners, developed by Dexino Cloud & AI Solutions, handles information when you use the application.

Our approach: The app is designed to provide a simple and welcoming way to learn short Qur'an Surahs, Salah, Wudu and basic Islamic knowledge while keeping data collection to a minimum.

1. Information collected by the app

Quran Learning for Beginners does not require users to create an account or sign in to use its core learning features.

Learning preferences, progress and certain application settings may be stored locally on the user's device so that the app can remember the user's experience between sessions.

Dexino does not use the app to directly collect names, postal addresses, personal messages, contact lists or other similar personal information from users.

2. Advertising

The free version of Quran Learning for Beginners may display advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

The advertising service may automatically process technical information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, general device information, interactions with ads, diagnostic information and information required for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

Advertising is intentionally restricted within the app. Advertisements are not intended to interrupt Qur'an recitation, individual Qur'an Ayah lessons, Salah instruction or Wudu instruction.

Where required by applicable law, users may be presented with privacy and advertising consent choices before advertising services are used.

3. Ad-free purchase

The app offers an optional one-time purchase that removes advertisements.

Payments are processed through Google Play. Dexino does not receive or store the user's complete payment card or banking details.

Google Play may process purchase information necessary to complete the transaction and to allow the app to determine whether the user owns the ad-free entitlement.

4. Third-party services

Quran Learning for Beginners uses services provided by Google, including services that may include:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads
  • Google User Messaging Platform for advertising consent
  • Google Play services
  • Google Play Billing for in-app purchases

These services may process data according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at Google Privacy Policy .

5. Data sharing

Dexino does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers such as Google where this is necessary to provide advertising, purchase processing, fraud prevention, diagnostics or related application functionality.

6. Data security

Dexino takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the application and to minimise unnecessary collection of user information.

Information transmitted by third-party services is subject to the security measures and privacy practices of those service providers.

7. Children and younger users

Quran Learning for Beginners uses a simple and accessible learning design, but the application is primarily intended for new Muslims, beginners and people who are curious about Islam. It is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

If the intended audience or data practices of the application change in a future release, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

8. International users

The app may be used internationally. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the user's country of residence, subject to their applicable privacy safeguards.

Dexino operates from Switzerland and aims to handle personal information in accordance with applicable Swiss data protection requirements and, where applicable, European data protection requirements.

9. Your choices

Depending on your device, region and applicable law, you may have choices regarding advertising consent, advertising identifiers and other privacy settings.

Android users can also manage certain privacy and advertising controls through their device and Google account settings.

Users who purchase the lifetime ad-free option will no longer be shown Dexino's in-app advertisements while the entitlement is active.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, third-party services, legal requirements or data practices change.

The latest version will remain available on this page.

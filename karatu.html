Karatu – Read PDFs and EPUBs, or Listen with Text-to-Speech

PDF Reader · EPUB Reader · Read Aloud · TTS Reader · Android

Karatu is an Android reading and listening app designed for people who want a simple way to read PDF and EPUB books and switch between reading and listening.

Import your own PDF and EPUB books or documents, keep them in your personal library, and continue from exactly where you stopped.

Get Karatu on Google Play

Read. Listen. Continue.
Karatu combines PDF and EPUB reading with Android text-to-speech and automatic reading-position memory.

PDF and EPUB reader for Android

Add PDF and EPUB files to Karatu and read them directly inside the app. Your library, book metadata and reading progress stay available so you can return to your books whenever you want.

Read books aloud with text-to-speech

Karatu can use your Android device's text-to-speech system to read supported book and document text aloud. Follow along on screen while listening, then switch back to normal reading whenever you prefer.

Continue where you stopped

Karatu automatically remembers your reading position. Stop reading or listening and return later without having to search for your place again.

Karatu features

  • Read PDF files
  • Read EPUB books
  • Text-to-speech read-aloud
  • Follow along while Karatu reads
  • Automatic reading-position memory
  • Personal book library
  • Local reading progress and metadata
  • Add content from files, links and Android sharing
  • Simple, distraction-free reading experience

Built for everyday reading and listening

Karatu is useful when you want to read an ebook, listen to a document, study while following the text, or simply continue a book later without complicated setup.

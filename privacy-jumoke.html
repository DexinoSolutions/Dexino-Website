Jumoke

Privacy Policy

Dexino Cloud & AI Solutions · Mobile Apps · Privacy

This Privacy Policy explains how Jumoke, developed by Dexino Cloud & AI Solutions, handles information when you use the application.

Privacy-conscious design: Jumoke is designed primarily as a local-first visual wardrobe and outfit organiser. Wardrobe photographs and clothing images are normally stored on the user's device rather than uploaded to Dexino. The application may, however, use third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads and external websites, which may process certain device, advertising, diagnostic or usage information.

1. Data controller

For data processing carried out directly by Jumoke or Dexino, the responsible provider is:

2. Camera access

Jumoke may request access to the device camera so that users can photograph clothing, accessories and other wardrobe items.

Camera access is used only when the user initiates functionality that requires the camera.

Jumoke does not use photographs for facial recognition, biometric identification or identity verification.

3. Photos, media and wardrobe images

Users may photograph, select, crop, edit, organise and delete wardrobe images using Jumoke.

Under the current local-first design, these wardrobe images are normally stored through Android device storage or media functionality, including device photo or gallery storage.

Dexino does not directly upload or store users' wardrobe photographs on Dexino servers as part of Jumoke's current core wardrobe functionality.

Dexino does not claim ownership of photographs or other content created or selected by users.

4. Local application data

Jumoke may store information locally on the user's device, including:

  • wardrobe image references;
  • selected clothing and outfit information;
  • application preferences;
  • shopping or editing state required by application features; and
  • other technical information required for the application to function.

This information is primarily used to provide Jumoke's application features and is not intentionally uploaded to Dexino servers unless a future feature expressly states otherwise.

5. Device permissions

Depending on the Android version and functionality used, Jumoke may request access to the camera, photographs, media or storage.

Permissions are requested to enable the corresponding feature. Users may manage application permissions through their device settings, although disabling a required permission may prevent the related feature from functioning.

6. Accounts and information provided directly to Dexino

Jumoke does not currently require an account or sign-in to use its core wardrobe functionality.

Dexino does not use Jumoke's core functionality to directly request users' names, postal addresses, contact lists or private messages.

If a user voluntarily contacts Dexino by email or another support channel, Dexino may receive the user's email address, message content and any information the user voluntarily provides. This information may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain business records, resolve disputes or comply with legal obligations.

7. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

Jumoke may display advertising using Google Mobile Ads or Google AdMob services.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process certain information, which may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device or advertising identifiers;
  • application and advertisement interactions;
  • diagnostic and performance information;
  • app launch, tap or similar interaction information; and
  • information used for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention.

This information may be processed by Google independently of Dexino's local wardrobe functionality.

Advertising may be personalised or non-personalised depending on the user's region, device settings, consent status, applicable law and Google advertising requirements.

Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy .

8. Shopping browser and external websites

Jumoke may provide functionality that allows users to browse or interact with third-party websites, including online shops.

When a user visits an external website, that website may independently receive or collect information such as the user's IP address, cookies, device information, account information or browsing interactions. Such processing is governed by the privacy policy and terms of the relevant third party.

Dexino does not control the privacy practices, security, content, availability or data handling of third-party websites.

Users should review the privacy policies of external websites before providing personal information to them.

9. User-initiated sharing and external transfers

If a user deliberately exports, shares, uploads or otherwise transfers an image or other content to another application, website or person, the resulting transfer is initiated by the user.

Once information is transferred to a third party, the third party's own terms and privacy practices may apply.

10. Data sharing

Dexino does not sell users' wardrobe photographs or personal information.

User wardrobe photographs are not intentionally transmitted to Dexino as part of the application's current local wardrobe functionality.

Certain technical, advertising or device information may nevertheless be processed or shared by third-party services integrated into the application, including Google Mobile Ads, as described in this Privacy Policy.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, enforce legal rights, investigate fraud or security issues, or respond to lawful requests from competent authorities.

11. International data processing

Third-party service providers used by Jumoke, including Google, may process information in countries other than the user's country of residence.

Where applicable, such processing is subject to the safeguards, contractual arrangements and legal mechanisms used by the relevant service provider and required by applicable data-protection law.

12. Data retention and deletion

Application-private information stored locally may be removed when the application is uninstalled, subject to Android functionality, device backups and operating-system behaviour.

Wardrobe photographs stored in the Android photo gallery, MediaStore or another shared device location may remain on the device even after Jumoke is uninstalled.

Users should use Jumoke's delete functionality or their device's photo or file-management functions when they want such images permanently removed.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to the retention policies of those providers.

13. Data security

Dexino uses reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to limit unnecessary collection, processing and transmission of user information.

No software, storage system or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their device, including through appropriate device passwords, PINs, biometrics or other security controls.

14. User rights

Depending on applicable data-protection law, users may have rights regarding personal data processed about them, including rights to request information, access, correction, deletion, restriction or other remedies provided by law.

Users may contact Dexino at info@dexino.com to make a privacy-related request concerning information controlled by Dexino.

Where information is processed independently by a third-party provider, users may need to exercise their rights directly with that provider.

15. Children's privacy

Jumoke is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children.

The application does not intentionally request children to provide personal information directly to Dexino through its core wardrobe functionality.

Parents and guardians should supervise use of the application where appropriate and manage device, advertising and privacy settings available to them.

16. Changes to Jumoke

Jumoke may evolve over time. If features such as accounts, cloud storage, synchronisation, analytics, purchases or other functionality materially change the application's data practices, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Dexino may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Jumoke, legal requirements, third-party services or business practices.

The current version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

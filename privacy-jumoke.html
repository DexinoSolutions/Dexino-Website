Jumoke

Privacy Policy

Dexino Cloud & AI Solutions · Mobile Apps · Privacy

This Privacy Policy explains how Jumoke, developed by Dexino Cloud & AI Solutions, handles information when you use the application.

Our approach: Jumoke is designed as a privacy-conscious visual wardrobe organiser. Users can photograph and organise hats, tops, trousers and shoes to create and compare outfits.

1. Camera access

Jumoke may request permission to use the device camera so that users can photograph clothing and outfit items.

Camera access is used only when required for functionality initiated by the user.

2. Photos and wardrobe images

Images created or selected for Jumoke are used to provide the wardrobe and outfit-management features of the application.

Under the current local-first design, wardrobe images are intended to remain on the user's device unless the user explicitly chooses a feature that exports, shares or otherwise transfers an image.

Dexino does not claim ownership of photographs created or stored by users.

3. Local application data

Information such as wardrobe categories, image references, selected outfits and application preferences may be stored locally on the device.

4. Accounts and personal information

Jumoke does not require an account or sign-in to use its core wardrobe-management functionality.

Dexino does not use the application to directly collect users' names, addresses, contact lists or personal messages.

5. Device permissions

Depending on the Android version and selected functionality, Jumoke may request permissions for the camera, photos or media storage.

Permissions are used only to provide the corresponding application feature.

6. Third-party services

The app may use Google Play and Android platform services required for application distribution, updates, security and compatible device functionality.

Google's privacy policy is available at Google Privacy Policy .

7. Data sharing

Dexino does not sell users' personal information or wardrobe photographs.

User images are not intentionally transmitted to Dexino as part of the application's core local wardrobe functionality.

8. Data security

Users should protect access to their mobile device because locally stored photographs may be accessible to someone who gains access to the device.

Dexino uses reasonable technical measures intended to minimise unnecessary collection and transmission of user content.

9. User control

Users can remove wardrobe items from the application using its available delete functionality.

Removing or uninstalling the application may also remove locally stored application information, depending on the device and Android backup settings.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if Jumoke introduces cloud storage, accounts, advertising, analytics or other functionality that changes its data practices.

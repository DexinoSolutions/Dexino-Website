Quran Learning for Beginners

Privacy Policy

Dexino Cloud & AI Solutions · Mobile Apps · Privacy

This Privacy Policy explains how Quran Learning for Beginners, developed by Dexino Cloud & AI Solutions, handles information when you use the application.

Our approach: Quran Learning for Beginners is designed as a simple and welcoming learning application for new Muslims, beginners and people who are curious about Islam, while keeping unnecessary data collection to a minimum.

1. Information collected by the app

The app does not require users to create a Dexino account or sign in to use its core learning features.

Learning progress, preferences and application settings may be stored locally on the user's device so that the app can remember progress between sessions.

Dexino does not use the app to directly collect names, postal addresses, contact lists or personal messages.

2. Advertising

The free version of Quran Learning for Beginners may display advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Google advertising services may process technical information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, interactions with advertisements, diagnostics and information used for fraud prevention and advertising delivery.

Advertising is intentionally restricted within the application. Advertisements are not intended to appear inside individual Quran Ayah lessons, during Quran recitation, during Salah instruction or during Wudu instruction.

Where required by applicable law, users may be presented with privacy or advertising consent choices.

3. Remove Ads purchase

The application offers an optional one-time purchase that removes advertisements.

Purchases are processed through Google Play. Dexino does not receive or store users' complete credit card or banking details.

Google Play may process purchase information required to complete the transaction and verify whether the user owns the ad-free entitlement.

4. Quran and learning content

The app provides Quran learning, human Quran recitation, Salah, Wudu and introductory Islamic learning content.

Core learning content does not require users to provide personal information.

5. Third-party services

Quran Learning for Beginners may use services provided by Google, including:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads
  • Google advertising consent services
  • Google Play services
  • Google Play Billing

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Google's privacy policy is available at Google Privacy Policy .

6. Data sharing

Dexino does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers such as Google where necessary to provide advertising, purchase processing, fraud prevention, security or other application functionality.

7. Data security

Dexino uses reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to minimise unnecessary collection of information and protect the application.

8. Children and younger users

The application uses an accessible and beginner-friendly design, but it is primarily intended for new Muslims, beginners and people who are curious about Islam.

The application is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

9. International users

Quran Learning for Beginners may be used internationally.

Dexino operates from Switzerland and aims to handle information in accordance with applicable Swiss data protection requirements and, where applicable, European data protection requirements.

10. Your choices

Depending on the user's location, device and applicable law, users may have options regarding advertising consent and advertising identifiers.

Users may also purchase the lifetime Remove Ads option to use the application without Dexino's in-app advertisements.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if application functionality, third-party services, legal requirements or data practices change.

The latest version will remain available on this page.

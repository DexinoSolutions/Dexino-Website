      <h1>How AI Can Help SMEs Without Risking Sensitive Data</h1>
      <p>AI can help small businesses, but it should be introduced carefully. Sensitive company data, customer information and internal documents need clear access control.</p>
<h2>Start with low-risk use cases</h2><p>Good first projects include internal document search, meeting summaries, policy Q&A and simple process automation.</p>
<h2>Keep data governance clear</h2><p>Before connecting AI to company documents, define who may access which content and which systems are allowed to process the data.</p>
<h2>Use AI where it saves time</h2><p>The goal is not hype. The goal is practical support for real workflows such as support requests, document handling and knowledge management.</p>

Hausa Learning App

Privacy Policy

Dexino Cloud & AI Solutions · Mobile Apps · Privacy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hausa Learning App, developed by Dexino Cloud & AI Solutions, handles information when you use the application.

Our approach: The Hausa Learning App is designed as a privacy-conscious learning application for studying Hausa vocabulary, phrases, sentences, dialogues and pronunciation.

1. Information collected by the app

The Hausa Learning App does not require an account or sign-in to use its core learning features.

Learning progress, preferences, daily goals and application settings may be stored locally on the user's device so that the application can remember progress between sessions.

Dexino does not use the app to directly collect names, postal addresses, contact lists, personal messages or similar personal information.

2. Learning and audio content

The application contains learning material and audio designed to help users study Hausa pronunciation, vocabulary and language usage.

Most learning content required by the application is bundled with or stored by the app to support offline learning.

3. Device permissions

Certain Android permissions may be used where required for audio playback and other application functionality.

The application does not use those permissions to collect unrelated personal information.

4. Third-party services

The application may use standard Google Play and Android platform services required for distribution, security, updates and compatible device functionality.

These services are governed by the privacy policies and terms of their respective providers.

Google's privacy policy is available at Google Privacy Policy .

5. Data sharing

Dexino does not sell users' personal information.

Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to provide platform services, comply with applicable law or protect the security of the application and its users.

6. Data security

Dexino uses reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to minimise unnecessary data collection and protect application data.

7. Children and younger learners

The Hausa Learning App uses a simple and accessible learning design that may also be suitable for families and younger learners.

The app does not require children to create an account or provide personal profile information in order to use its core learning content.

8. International users

The Hausa Learning App may be used internationally. Dexino operates from Switzerland and aims to handle information in accordance with applicable Swiss data protection requirements and, where applicable, European data protection requirements.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features, third-party services or data practices change.

The latest version will remain available on this page.
